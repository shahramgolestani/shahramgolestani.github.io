I am Shahram Golestani, an economist and researcher with a Ph.D. in Economics.

My academic and research interests include political economy, monetary systems, energy geopolitics, game theory, econometrics, and global economic structures.

My work focuses on understanding how economic systems interact with political power, institutions, and international strategic dynamics.

This website is a platform for my research, essays, and ongoing analytical work.
